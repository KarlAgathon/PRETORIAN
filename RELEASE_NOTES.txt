Media Center 2.3.0

• Works on phones and tablets too: tap the picture to show or hide the controls, tap the icons, press and hold = long OK
• Home: Continue watching, Recently watched and Favourites appear at the top only when they have something in them
• Top bar on one line: playlist · expiry date · days left (green, orange, red; grey when the provider gives no date), bell with unread count, time and date
• Notification center: newest first, OK opens the place it is about, hold OK removes one, Clear notifications with a confirmation
• Live TV: the sixth icon is now Settings (gear) with Channel info, Audio, Subtitles, Sleep timer, External player, Time shift and EPG settings – only what works for that channel
• Live TV format: Auto, Fit, Zoom, Stretch
• Other channels with the same programme: "Available (n)" at the top right
• One Subtitles menu everywhere: Provider on top, Online below, only language and version (English (V1), English (V2)) – no file or release names
• Film page: the first online subtitle is downloaded in the background, so it is ready at once
• Film and series player settings: subtitle delay and audio delay
• Series › Streaming: the services' own logos
• Search: results from the first letter; the last 10 searches per category, each can be removed, Clear history with a confirmation
• Catch-up: shows are searched in the guide kept on the box (up to 7 days back), on every archive channel at once, results while you type; the panel shows where you are
• Settings in eight categories: Playlists, Playback, Appearance & Language, Home & Navigation, Parental control, Sports center, Notifications & Reminders, System & Maintenance
• Only one playlist is active: switching one on switches the others off
• Adding a playlist: form on the left, phone QR always on the right; Done on the phone fills the fields and waits on Save; connection errors in plain words
• Loading a playlist ends with "The library is ready" and Enter Media Center; a part the provider does not offer shows Not available
• Lite looks the same as Full (focus zoom, team crests, soft poster backgrounds, fades); Lite below 3 GB RAM; the speed test no longer runs on the main thread; Settings shows which mode is really running

Media Center 2.2.0

• Movies and Series, one menu at a time: the menu disappears when a category opens, Back returns to the same place
  – Movies: Continue watching, Recently added, In cinemas, My List, then the provider's groups
  – Series: Continue watching, Recently added, Streaming, My List, then the provider's groups
• Categories open as a full-screen browser: the selected title fills the top (picture, story, Watch · Trailer · My List), posters below
• Continue watching: OK resumes at once; hold OK for Continue, Play from the beginning, Remove (the progress is kept)
• In cinemas and Streaming (Netflix, Apple TV+, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max, Paramount+, Peacock, Hulu, Starz) show only titles that are in your playlist
• My List: one press adds or removes, the bookmark fills white, a short note confirms
• Film page: big picture, three actions, Direction · Cast · Audio · Subtitles (provider) · Subtitles (online), Similar movies from your playlist
• Series page: Continue Sx Ex, Trailer, My List, season cards with progress; a season opens its episodes (watched, new, not out yet)
• Hold OK on an episode: Episode details, Mark as watched / unwatched, Remove from Continue watching
• Film and series player: seven icons (Play, back, forward, Subtitles, Audio, Format, Settings)
• Subtitle menu: Provider tracks and Online versions in your language (V1, V2, V3), Find more subtitles
• Player settings: subtitle language, audio, subtitle font and size, skip forward and back (5–30 s), display format; for series also autoplay next episode, skip intro, skip recap
• Online subtitles are searched already on the film page, so they are ready when you press Watch
• Live TV full screen: with the controls open, other channels of your playlist with the same match or programme can be chosen (top right)
