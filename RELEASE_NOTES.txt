Media Center 2.0.0

A fresh start: new name, new look, simpler from the first screen.
• New identity: Media Center, new icon and banner
• One design for everyone: Monochrome Silver (black, charcoal, silver, white)
• Home is only the menu: TV, Movies, Series, Catch-up, Search, Settings
• Small numbers beside Movies and Series show new titles; hold OK there to clear them
• Sport is now the first row inside TV
• First-time setup: language, legal notice, then add a playlist or skip
• Search by category: TV, Movies, Series, Sport and Settings
• Continue Watching is always first in Movies and Series
• Live TV bar with six actions: Pause, Favourite, EPG, Format, Archive, More
• Calm messages: "Channel unavailable", no warnings when a guide is missing, a small note when there is no internet
• Settings in six groups
• Removed: screensaver, simple mode, profiles, icon sets, theme choices, AI broadcaster estimate
• Security: playlist logins encrypted on the box, stronger backup encryption, backups without a password no longer contain logins, safer phone pairing, crash logs without passwords, updates only over https

Note: this version starts with a clean setup. Old backups from earlier versions cannot be restored.
